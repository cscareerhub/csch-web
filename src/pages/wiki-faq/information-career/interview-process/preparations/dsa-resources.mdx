<h1 className={styles.bigTitle}>DS&A Resources</h1>

Improving your Data Structures & Algorithms (DS&A) skills is one of the best ways to stand out in technical interviews. Below are some of the most popular and effective resources — from practice platforms to video lectures, books, and cheat sheets.

---

## Practice Platforms

- LeetCode: The go-to platform for coding interview prep; offers problems sorted by difficulty, topic, and company tags.
- HackerRank: Beginner-friendly, with structured tutorials and challenges across topics.
- Codeforces: Competitive programming site; good for building speed and problem-solving under pressure.
- Exercism: Mentor-guided practice problems in various languages.
- InterviewBit: Combines practice problems with structured interview prep.

## Video Courses

- CS50's Introduction to Computer Science (Harvard): Covers foundational programming and algorithmic thinking.
- NeetCode YouTube Channel: Excellent breakdowns of common LeetCode problems with step-by-step reasoning.
- Abdul Bari's Algorithms Playlist: Great visual explanations of core algorithm concepts.

## Books

- Cracking the Coding Interview by Gayle Laakmann McDowell: Still the gold standard for interview prep.
- Grokking Algorithms by Aditya Bhargava: Visual, beginner-friendly introduction to algorithms.
- Elements of Programming Interviews (EPI): Great for deeper problem-solving practice.

## Cheat Sheets & References

- Big-O Cheat Sheet: Quick reference for time and space complexities.
- Visualgo: Interactive visualizations of data structures and algorithms.
- GeeksforGeeks: In-depth tutorials and example problems for every DS&A topic.
- Tech Interview Handbook: Condensed notes for last-minute review.

💡 Tip: Don't just read or watch — implement each data structure or algorithm in your preferred programming language, then solve problems that require it. Active practice builds real recall.
