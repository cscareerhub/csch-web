# General Career Resources

Useful online resources for skill-building, job searching, and career development.

- [LinkedIn Learning](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/): Professional courses on a wide range of career, technical, and soft skills.
- [Coursera Career Academy](https://www.coursera.org/): Industry-recognized certifications and career-focused courses.
- [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/): Company reviews, salary ranges, and interview insights from current and former employees.
- [Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/): Compensation data and leveling guides for tech roles.
- [The Muse](https://www.themuse.com/): Career advice, job search tips, and workplace culture guides.
- [Workable's Interview Question Library](https://resources.workable.com/interview-questions): Common interview questions by role and skill.
- [O*NET Online](https://www.onetonline.org/): Official U.S. Department of Labor database with detailed job descriptions, skills, and outlook.
- [Skillshare](https://www.skillshare.com/): Creative and professional skill-building through community-driven video courses.
- [MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/): Free academic courses, including computer science and business topics.
- [CareerOneStop](https://www.careeronestop.org/): U.S. Department of Labor career exploration and training resource hub.
